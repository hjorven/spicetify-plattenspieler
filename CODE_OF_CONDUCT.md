# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want Plattenspieler to be a friendly project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; criticize code, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask before you judge.
- Respect that maintainers decide what fits the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminating or demeaning comments
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing other people's private information
- Trolling or repeatedly derailing discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people
who repeatedly or seriously break it.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/hjorven/spicetify-plattenspieler/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message to [@hjorven](https://github.com/hjorven). Reports are handled confidentially.
